## D. Curriculum / content audit (3 lessons)
Cross-check against authoritative methods resources was **partial**: key statistical claims were checked against the verified abstracts (Langan 2019, Veroniki 2016, Rücker 2008, Higgins & Thompson 2002, Borenstein 2010, Munn 2018, Schardt 2007). The **Cochrane Handbook, JBI Manual and PRISMA E&E full text were not re-read**; statements attributed to general "Cochrane/PRISMA practice" (e.g. Module 3 quiz Q1 explanation) are standard but unverified this session.
Known gaps / simplifications to fix next:
- M3 does not yet cover critical, integrative, realist, meta-narrative, bibliometric, methodological, mixed-methods or qualitative evidence syntheses beyond a mention; decision tree omits qualitative/diagnostic/prognostic branches.
- M4 gives PCC/SPIDER definitions from standard usage without a verified primary citation; no diagnostic/prognostic question templates in the builder (PICO/PECO/PCC only).
- M32 omits Mantel-Haenszel/Peto (Module 33), τ² confidence intervals (Q-profile; recommended by Veroniki 2016), the Knapp-Hartung truncation debate details, and small-sample prediction-interval caveats; the "random effects can amplify small-study bias" point is stated without a citation.
- Lessons contain far fewer than the 30 spec elements (missing: glossary links, interactive per-lesson demos, printable cheat sheet, mastery assessment beyond quiz, estimated-time validation).
- Quiz conceptual answers have **not** been reviewed by a qualified human.
