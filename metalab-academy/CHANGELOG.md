# Changelog
## 0.4.0 (2026-11-28)
- **Curriculum:** all 90 registered modules now have AI-drafted lessons (36 previously outline-only modules written; modules 89 and 90 added after auditing coverage against the 26 Cochrane Handbook v6 chapters - titles verified in Crossref, content not read). A mechanical lesson-depth audit shows 47 substantive and 43 thin drafts: the thin ones are the deepening backlog, shown in the app. Nothing is expert-reviewed.
- **Statistics (advanced2.py, benchmarked vs R/metafor 4.4.0, clubSandwich 0.5.10, base R):** trim-and-fill (L0/R0/Q0, with warnings), PET/PEESE, CR2 + Satterthwaite robust variance, unstructured multivariate and bivariate DTA models, contrast-based network meta-analysis (multi-arm, league table, P-scores with an explicit ranking-direction argument), phylogenetic multilevel model, Bayesian normal-normal meta-analysis by exact quadrature, power/required k, IPD two-stage, continuity-correction sensitivity, log-HR extraction, lnVR. Benchmark audit found and fixed a CR2 defect and four reference-script defects. netmeta/mada/bayesmeta/dosresmeta/metasens could not be installed: those methods are benchmarked against metafor rma.mv or independent integration, status `requires_external_validation`; R workflows for them are in validation/external_workflows/ (never executed by the developer).
- **End-to-end pipeline:** a fictional simulated review runs every stage (protocol, searches, import, dedup, screening + kappa, extraction + independent second-extractor verification, RoB, effects, sensitivity, stored results, manuscript tokens, export) with independent cross-checks (PRISMA recounted from the exported CSVs; analysis reproduced in R/metafor); a readiness checker and traceability report are in the Research Workspace.
- **Reference Encyclopedia:** 487 searchable entries (effect sizes, formulas, assumptions, mistakes, decision trees, interpretation examples, method comparisons, reporting guidelines, RoB instruments, troubleshooting, 14 R/Python commands executed against metafor in the tests).
- **Learning:** calculation drills, method-selection and interpretation banks, final comprehensive assessment (65 questions, sections + 18 curriculum parts), capstone rubric (13 items), 789 flashcards, practice-dataset ZIP, per-pathway formula sheets, internal learning record (explicitly NOT a certificate). Method wizard updated to say what v0.4 does and does not implement.
- **Knowledge Universe:** reading list for every module (verified core references + candidate guidance/courses/tutorials/datasets/events); 40 more candidate resources, 10 organisations, 5 communities, 8 directories; batch verification tool for the owner's machine (tools/verify_batch.py). All candidates remain unverified.
- **Networking:** personal local dashboard (interests, meetings, authorship discussions, mentor shortlist, opportunity bookmarks, outreach drafts, contribution tracking); no automatic contact; personal tables excluded from shareable backups; names never written to the audit log.
- **Expert review:** eight area packages (statistics, searching, SR methodology, risk of bias, education, disciplines, usability/accessibility, integrity/privacy) + per-lesson packages + ZIP with INDEX.
- **Verification records:** 142 bibliographically verified references (+45); 72 tracked claims (abstract-level where stated); 21 logged corrections including defects found by the benchmarks.
- **Quality:** 824 tests; browser audit (axe-core in Chromium) of 28 page/tab views: 1 minor violation in Streamlit's own file-uploader component; keyboard walk unchanged.
