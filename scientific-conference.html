About Drosie's First Scientific Conference

Scientists do not only run experiments. They also share what they found, listen to questions, and revise their ideas. Drosie's First Scientific Conference is a free, interactive experience where children ages 7 to 12 practice that whole process in a simulated, educational conference. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking, and your work stays on your own device.

What learners do

  1. The Conference Invitation: meet Drosie, learn why scientists attend conferences, and design a badge.
  2. Choose Your Research Path: investigate a simulated fruit fly climbing experiment, or bring your own project.
  3. The Research Investigation: run repeated trials in a virtual lab and record the numbers.
  4. Become a Data Detective: summarize data, read graphs and tell observations from interpretations.
  5. The Abstract Studio: write a five-part scientific abstract with live feedback.
  6. The Scientific Poster Studio: build, check, print or save a poster as a PDF.
  7. Registration and Preparation: check in at a fictional conference and plan a 60-second talk.
  8. The Interactive Conference Hall: explore six areas and talk with fictional scientists.
  9. Present, Discuss, and Revise: practice explaining results, answer audience questions and improve your work.
  10. The Conference Finale: get a participation certificate and a printable portfolio.

Science ideas you will practice

Research question versus hypothesis versus prediction; variables and controls; repeated measurements versus independent replicates; observations versus interpretations; uncertainty and limitations; and why it is fine to say "I don't know yet." The fruit fly data are simulated for learning, not a real experiment.
