Cue Maker: time Drosie's narration to the letter text

How to use:

  1. Choose the recorded narration file (MP3, M4A, WAV or OGG).
  2. Press Play. Each time Drosie starts the highlighted phrase, press Space or Mark.
  3. Fine-tune any time with the ± buttons, then Preview.
  4. Download letter-01.cues.json and put it in letters/assets/audio/ beside the audio file.
