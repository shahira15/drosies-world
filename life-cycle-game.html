🔬 Drosie's Science Corner

Egg: A tiny egg is the beginning of a new fruit fly's life.

Larva: A young fruit fly hatches as a worm-like larva. It eats and grows, shedding its outer covering as it develops.

Pupa: Inside its protective puparium, the fly changes into its adult form.

Adult: The grown-up fruit fly has six legs, two wings, and compound eyes. Adult flies can lay eggs to begin the cycle again!

Did you know? Fruit flies undergo complete metamorphosis: egg → larva → pupa → adult.
