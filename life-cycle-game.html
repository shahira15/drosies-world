      Science notes

      Fruit fly life cycle at about 25°C (77°F)
      StageAbout how longWhat happens
      Eggabout 1 dayThe embryo develops inside the egg, which is about 0.5 mm long and has two respiratory filaments.
      Larvaabout 4 daysLegless larva feeds and grows through three instars separated by molts. Late third-instar larvae wander away from food.
      Pupaabout 4–5 daysThe last larval skin hardens into the puparium. Metamorphosis happens inside.
      Adultemerges around day 9–10The adult emerges (eclosion), its wings expand and harden, and it can later reproduce.

      Timing changes with temperature, diet and genetic background. Cooler is slower and warmer is faster, within the range flies tolerate.

      Complete metamorphosis
      Egg, larva, pupa, adult: the larva and the adult look and live very differently.
      Instar
      A larval stage between molts. Fruit flies have three.
      Molt
      Shedding the outer skin (cuticle) so the larva can grow.
      Puparium
      The hard case formed from the last larval skin.
      Pupa
      The developing fly inside the puparium.
      Eclosion
      The adult emerging from the puparium.
      Halteres
      Tiny knob-like balancing organs that replace the second pair of wings in flies.

      About the pictures: the scenes are simplified illustrations. They are not real microscopy, a time-lapse recording, or a validated model of development.
