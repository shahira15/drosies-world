    Science notes

    Approximate fruit fly timeline (reference example)
    StageAt about 25°C
    Egg (embryo)about 1 day
    First instar larvaabout day 1–2
    Second instar larvaabout day 2–3
    Third instar larvaabout day 3–5
    Pupa in the pupariumabout day 5 to day 9–10
    Adult emerges (eclosion)around day 9–10

    Cooler temperatures slow development (roughly 19 days at 18°C) and warmer ones speed it up (roughly 7 days at 29°C). These are rough reference values; real timing also depends on food, crowding and the strain of fly.

    Culture
    A group of living organisms grown in a container on food, with labels and records.
    Nutrient
    A substance an organism needs to live and grow.
    Incubator
    A cabinet that keeps the temperature steady.
    Instar
    A larval stage between molts. Fruit flies have three.
    Molt
    Shedding the old outer skin so the larva can grow.
    Puparium
    The hard case formed from the last larval skin.
    Pupa
    The developing fly inside the puparium.
    Eclosion
    The adult coming out of the puparium.

    The food recipe in this game is one example of a cornmeal-based medium. Laboratories use many different recipes. Real fly food is prepared by trained adults using careful, clean procedures.
