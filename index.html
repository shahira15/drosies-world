✳ THE STORY BEHIND DROSIE

Science belongs
to everyone.

Drosie was inspired by a simple question from a child: “What do you do in your lab?”

Created by Shahira Helal Arzoo, a biology doctoral researcher and science outreach educator, Drosie brings the fascinating world of fruit flies into the hands of young readers. The project combines storytelling, real biology, and playful learning to help more children imagine themselves as scientists.

Whether you're a parent, teacher, museum educator, or simply a curious explorer, you're welcome here.
