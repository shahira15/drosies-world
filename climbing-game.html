What is a climbing assay?

Fruit flies often climb upward after being gently disturbed. Scientists call this negative geotaxis. In this playful demonstration, watch ten virtual flies move inside a vial and count how many reach the orange line.

Science note: This is a simplified simulation. Results are randomly generated for learning and are not real laboratory measurements.
