# Scientific Verification Matrix

Prepared 2026-11-24 for the v1.0.0 archival draft. Machine-readable copy: `SCIENTIFIC_VERIFICATION_MATRIX.csv`.

**How to read the status column.** `V-ABS`: the record was retrieved from PubMed in this session and its **abstract** was read; the full text was **not** read. `V-META`: the record's existence and bibliographic metadata were confirmed (PubMed or Crossref); content not read. `V-REC`: verified live against the Zenodo API. `UNV`: a source is cited in the repository but was not checked here. `NONE`: no source is cited or verified.

Literature records marked V-ABS were retrieved from **PubMed** (National Library of Medicine); each DOI below resolves to the article. "Supports" means *the cited abstract is consistent with the stated claim*; it does **not** mean an expert has confirmed that the simulation implements the source correctly. **No independent scientific review has been completed.**

- Discrepancies found: gene count (13,600 in source vs 'roughly 14,000' on page); Kosambi year (1944 in repository vs 1943 in Crossref); Miller 2018 citation (wrong title and journal in repository; corrected); 'balancer recombination completely suppressed' vs rare crossovers reported by Miller 2016/2018 (documented simplification).

## Miller 2018 investigation (unresolved reference)

1. The repository cited `Miller DE, Cook KR, Hawley RS et al. (2018) The evolutionary history of Drosophila balancer chromosomes. Genetics (PMC5873907)`. A PubMed citation lookup for that title and journal found nothing.
2. Converting the PMC identifier given in the repository, **PMC5873907**, returned **PMID 29420191, doi:10.1534/g3.118.200021**.
3. PubMed metadata for that record: *The Molecular and Genetic Characterization of Second Chromosome Balancers in Drosophila melanogaster*, Miller DE, Cook KR, Hemenway EA, Fang V, Miller AL, Hales KG, Hawley RS, **G3 (Bethesda) 2018;8(4):1161-1171**. The authors match; the journal and title in the repository were wrong. It concerns second-chromosome balancers including **CyO**, so it is relevant to the CyO balancer in the simulator.
4. No substitute citation was invented. Related Miller/Cook/Hawley balancer papers found by search (PMIDs 27172211, 31697682, 26903656, 32972999) are listed for the reviewer as candidates only; only PMID 27172211 and 31697682 are used in the matrix, marked as candidates.
5. **Still open:** whether this paper supports the specific CyO marker list in the repository (the full text was not read). `drosie-genetics-lab/docs/SCIENTIFIC_REFERENCE.md` and `trait-database.js` now carry the corrected citation with that caveat.
