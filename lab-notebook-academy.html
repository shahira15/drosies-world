Welcome!

This is an educational simulation for practice. It teaches general principles of good scientific recordkeeping and is not a legally compliant, validated or audit-trailed professional electronic laboratory notebook. Real laboratories follow their own institution's rules, and notebook formats vary by field. All practice data stays on this device (local storage). There are no accounts, trackers or ads, and it is not connected to other Drosie's World activities.
