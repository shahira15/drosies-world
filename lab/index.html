The Secret Fly Lab: a free fruit fly science game for kids ages 5–8

Join Drosie the fruit fly (Drosophila melanogaster) in a story-led science adventure. Start in the Safety Academy to learn how scientists stay safe, then step into the lab to find a stereomicroscope, learn its parts, look closely at a fly's body and solve a small observation mystery.

What children practise

  • Basic laboratory safety habits
  • Naming microscope parts and using one step by step
  • Observing a living thing closely and naming body parts: head, thorax, abdomen, wings, legs and antennae
  • Comparing flies and recording what they notice
