Practical A · Mapping b and vg University Fly Genetics

Name: ____________________   Date: __________

Testcross: b[1] vg[1]/+ + ♀ × b[1] vg[1]/b[1] vg[1] ♂. Simulated counts: wild type 412 · black vestigial 407 · black 88 · vestigial 93 (N = 1000).

  1. Which classes are parental? Which are recombinant? Explain from the mother's chromosomes.
  2. Estimate r and its standard error. Give a 95% confidence interval.
  3. The classical map distance is 18.5 cM (2-48.5 to 2-67.0). Why can an observed RF be smaller than the map distance?
  4. Predict the result if the heterozygote were the male. Explain.
  5. Rewrite the cross for the repulsion phase and predict all four classes using r = 0.177.
Key: 1 parental: wild type, black vestigial (the maternal homologs b vg and + +); recombinant: black, vestigial · 2 r = 181/1000 = 0.181, SE = √(0.181·0.819/1000) = 0.0122, 95% CI ≈ 0.157–0.205 · 3 multiple crossovers are undetected over longer intervals; map distance sums short intervals (mapping functions) · 4 only parental classes, ½ each — no crossing over in Drosophila males · 5 b[1] +/+ vg[1] ♀ × b[1] vg[1] testers: black 0.4115, vestigial 0.4115, wild type 0.0885, black vestigial 0.0885.

Practical B · Lethals and balancers University Fly Genetics

Name: ____________________   Date: __________

  1. Cy[1]/+ intercross: give zygote genotype proportions and adult phenotype proportions.
  2. Data: 212 Curly : 88 flat. Test 3:1 and 2:1 (show χ², df, p). What do you conclude? What sample size would help?
  3. l(2)DEMO[1]/CyO intercross: list zygote classes, which survive, and the adult phenotype ratio.
  4. Give three properties that make CyO a useful balancer, and two features of real balancers the simulator does not model.
  5. Design a two-generation scheme to establish a balanced stock of a new chromosome 2 lethal l(2)new induced in a male.
Key: 1 zygotes ¼ Cy/Cy (die) : ½ Cy/+ : ¼ +/+; adults ⅔ Curly : ⅓ flat · 2 3:1 χ² = 3.004, p = 0.083; 2:1 χ² = 2.160, p = 0.142 — neither rejected at α = 0.05; N = 300 lacks power to separate them (several hundred to ~1000 more flies) · 3 ¼ l/l ✝, ½ l/CyO, ¼ CyO/CyO ✝ → 100% Curly adults · 4 dominant marker (Cy), recessive lethal, suppressed recovery of crossovers; not modeled: inversion breakpoints/aneuploid products, distal exchange (e.g. sp onto CyO), cn², pr¹ markers, fitness costs · 5 e.g. mutagenized ♂ × Sco/CyO ♀ (Sco not modeled) → single F1 l(2)new/CyO males × CyO-balancer females → intercross F2 l(2)new/CyO siblings; confirm lethality by absence of non-Curly progeny.
