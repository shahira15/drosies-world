Free fruit fly genetics simulator

Drosie Genetics Lab is a free, browser-based simulator where students breed virtual Drosophila melanogaster, predict offspring with Punnett squares, explore chromosomes and run small experiments with classic traits such as white eyes, curly wings and vestigial wings. Choose a level for ages 5–8, 9–12 or older students and teachers.

All offspring data are simulated using inheritance rules documented in the scientific reference, with alleles checked against FlyBase. For lesson plans, printable worksheets and answer keys see the teacher resources, or browse all science activities. The simulator needs JavaScript; it is loading…
